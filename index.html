Notes

A few honest answers.

How many are there?

3,333 in Series I. Each one is a single NFT drawn from its own seed.

What decides my boat?

A seed picks the hull, sail color, sea and storm strength. The same seed always draws the same boat.

Where does the art live?

Each Stromboat is a fixed pixel artwork tied to its seed. The storm preview in Article I is a toy sketch of the weather, not the art.

What comes with it?

An artwork. No token promises, no roadmap.
